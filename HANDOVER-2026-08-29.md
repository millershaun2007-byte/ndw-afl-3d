## Rules that keep being broken

**One change. One build. HE PLAYS IT. Then the next.** 14 commits in 67 minutes
with no playtest between any of them is what produced the defects above.
If you have made two commits without a playtest, stop and deploy.

- Say which claim you mean: **builds / deploys / screenshot-verified /
  human-played**. They are not interchangeable. Automated checks mash the button
  continuously, so they always win the ruck and only ever exercise the attacking
  path. Shaun loses rucks and then watches.
- Do not rebuild a commit and call it a restored build. `BuildSceneContents`
  regenerates the scene, so a rebuild is different bytes (31,102,400 archived vs
  31,120,864 rebuilt). Use the reference slots.
- Do not set `maxChainDepth` above 1 without asking. Killed twice by playtest.
- Do not raise `managedStrippingLevel` without `[Preserve]`/`link.xml` in the
  same commit — it strips `TapPressed` and the button goes dead with no error.
- Do not deploy with a bare `gsutil cp`. Use `sync-to-gcs.sh`, or the build is
  invisible to anyone who has played before (Unity caches by URL in IndexedDB).
