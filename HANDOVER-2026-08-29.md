  regenerates the scene, so a rebuild is different bytes. The originals are in
  the reference slots listed above.
- Do not set `maxChainDepth` above 1 without asking. Killed twice by playtest.

  same commit. It strips `TapPressed` and the button goes dead with no error.

  invisible to anyone who has played before.
